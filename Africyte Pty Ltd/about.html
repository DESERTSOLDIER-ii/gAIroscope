About Africyte

Learn about our mission, vision, and the team behind Africa's digital transformation

Our Mission

To create accessible digital opportunities for people who need them most — empowering underprivileged communities, small traders, and underserved entrepreneurs with tools, platforms, and support that turn skills and services into sustainable livelihoods.

Our Vision

To see an inclusive digital economy where everyone who has talent or a trade can access markets, grow businesses, and build wealth — powered by locally grown technology that meets global standards.

Our Story

Africyte (Pty) Ltd is a South African technology company redefining access to the digital economy. Founded under the holding of Pule Property, Africyte represents a new era of African innovation — a blend of purpose, progress, and precision.

We recognized that many skilled people in Africa remain excluded from formal trade because they lack digital tools and visibility. Africyte bridges that gap — transforming informal trade into sustainable entrepreneurship through technology, transparency, and trust.

Unlike traditional tech companies focused solely on profits, we build ecosystems that make markets fair, accessible, and profitable for everyone. Our "We Share Technology" philosophy ensures that value flows directly to those creating it.
