Our Hiring Process

We make joining our team straightforward and transparent

1. Submit Your CV

Email your CV to careers@africyte.co.za with the position you're applying for in the subject line.

2. Initial Screening

We'll contact you for a brief phone conversation to discuss your experience and interests.

3. Skills Assessment

For technical roles, we may ask you to complete a practical assessment relevant to the position.

4. Team Interview

Meet with our team to discuss how you can contribute to our mission and culture.

5. Offer & Onboarding

Successful candidates receive an offer and go through our comprehensive onboarding process.

Note: We aim to respond to all applications within 5 business days. Due to high volume, we may not be able to provide individual feedback to all applicants.
