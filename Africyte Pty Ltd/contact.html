Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What is Africyte's main focus?

Africyte focuses on creating accessible digital opportunities that empower underprivileged communities, small traders, and underserved entrepreneurs. We build platforms and tools that help turn skills and services into sustainable livelihoods.

How can I become a seller on Locallo?

To become a Locallo seller, please email info@locallo.co.za with your business details. Our team will guide you through the verification process and help you set up your digital storefront with all the necessary tools for success.

Do you offer custom software development?

Yes! Through our DEVFLOW studio, we offer custom software development tailored to your specific business needs. Contact info@devflow.co.za to discuss your project requirements and get a consultation.

What is your response time for inquiries?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call +27 79 244 2008 during business hours (GMT+2).

Are you currently hiring?

We're always looking for talented individuals who share our mission. Current openings are listed on our Careers page. Send your CV to careers@africyte.co.za to be considered for future opportunities as well.

Do you offer IT support for small businesses?

Absolutely! Our IT & Support services are designed for businesses of all sizes. We provide everything from basic technical support to comprehensive infrastructure management. Contact info@africyte.co.za to discuss your IT needs.
